Privacy policy

My rclone is used only by its owner, to upload the owner's own files from their own computers to their own Google Drive with the open-source tool rclone.

  • It accesses Google Drive only to upload, list and check the owner's own files.
  • It doesn't collect, store, sell or share anyone's personal data.
  • Its Google sign-in details stay on the owner's computers and are never sent anywhere except Google.
  • It has no other users. Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Questions: contact the owner through github.com/Ring-Master.
